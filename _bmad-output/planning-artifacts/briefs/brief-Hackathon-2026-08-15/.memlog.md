- (decision) Prototype platform: Electron desktop application with SQLite persistence
- (decision) Prototype uses mock data; Outlook, real OT/MUREX/filesystem integrations, and VBA-parity validation are out of scope for this phase
- (decision) Prototype flow: choose as-of date; reconcile mock broker and OT/MUREX data; review matched/unmatched/missing records in grids; comment; prepare broker email drafts; save a report
- (decision) Every unmatched record requires human review; comments should persist in SQLite
- (assumption) VBA mapping parity is a future production-phase concern, not a prototype gate
- (decision) Primary prototype objective: impress Adhi and business stakeholders in a hackathon pitch through modern, high-quality UI/UX
- (decision) Pitch quality requires a working prototype that presents the data users need, not visual polish alone
- (decision) Prototype trade grid must expose counterparty, ISIN, buy/sell, amount, quantity, currency, settlement date, and all other trade-relevant fields needed for review
- (decision) Trade grid opens with a curated priority-column set; users can reveal remaining trade fields without leaving the reconciliation screen
- (decision) Use a side detail panel for an unmatched trade: preserve the grid context while exposing full trade data, comments, and broker-email action
- (decision) After each reconciliation, show a quick summary and a lightweight historical anomaly signal based on persisted mock run history in SQLite
- (decision) Historical anomaly detection appears as a non-blocking warning in the post-reconciliation summary
- (decision) Prototype does not include a history drill-down; anomaly warning is limited to current-run explanation
- (decision) Landing experience: dashboard with the latest-run summary and a dominant Start Reconciliation action; provide an empty state before the first run
- (change) Drafted the prototype product brief from the session decisions; retained an explicit assumption that the pitch is judged primarily through a two- to three-minute end-to-end demo
- (event) Memlog audit: all meaningful decisions are captured in brief.md; no addendum is required. Applied structure and prose review, then marked the brief ready for review.
